Where the fuel goes

Linear generator force

Stators have cobalt-iron cores and aluminium windings, and the outer stator stops short of the outer dead centre where little force is needed. The machine carries all of the work and can finish a compression stroke by itself with the chamber vented. The peak gas load is carried by piston inertia, as in every free-piston engine; no machine this size could hold it, and none needs to.

Mass estimate

Built up from this geometry with ordinary material densities, about ±20 %. The first build of this engine (154 × 124 mm, copper windings, steel liners) was about 838 kg for the engine and 1,188 kg for the package.

Propane cooling

Six piston circuits plus two auxiliary circuits. Each chamber pumps its own loop: liquid enters through a check valve at the low end of the pressure swing, runs through that piston's half of the cooling gallery in the centre block, then sprays the crown underside and the stator slots, and the vapour leaves through the bleed valve to that circuit's condenser sector. The bleed valve sets the chamber pressure, which sets the boiling temperature and the return force together.
