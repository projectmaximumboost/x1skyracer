## 1.0 Mission & Philosophy

X1Racing™ is the world's first grand-prix series for manned-capable flying race cars. All races are 100% remotely piloted (zero onboard human risk) with mandatory SI (Super Intelligence) augmentation for safety and performance.

Remote operation enables risk-free collisions, maximum performance, and maximum competition — machines can sustain full-throttle aggression, extreme G-forces, repeated high-impact contact, deliberate ramming, and boundary-pushing maneuvers without endangering human life, creating racing that is faster, closer, and more intense than any traditional or manned series.

Core Mantra: Maximum Boost — unlimited power, maximum performance, maximum entertainment, maximum safety.

By removing the pilot from the cockpit, we unlock extreme 3D aerial maneuvers, true aerial combat, and sustained peak performance impossible in conventional motorsport.

Our mission is to accelerate real-world Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) technology through fierce competition, practical consumer tech transfer, and unforgettable spectacle.

## 2.0 Series Format

▸

**Championship:** X1 Cup (multi-round season). The Pilot Championship and Constructor Championship are titles within the X1 Cup.

▸

**Race Types:** Sprint races, Feature races, Endurance events, and Special formats (Pursuit, Elimination, Mixed Reality Challenges).

▸

**Tracks:** 3D air circuits (urban canyons, mountain passes, stadium loops, coastal courses) — both real geo-mapped and synthetic.

▸

**Grid & Heats:** Maximum 22 machines per heat. Events exceeding 22 entries are divided into multiple heats; top finishers advance to the final race.

▸

**Race Starts:** Standing start (zero speed on ground or hover) or in-air start (formation managed by X1 SI Boost layer). Start type is chosen by Race Control per event.

▸

**Race Duration:** 22–44 minutes plus one lap. Exact duration is set by Race Control for each event.

▸

Events are run remotely/SI-augmented only (manned mode limited to future demos or certification testing).

## 3.0 Technical Specifications – X1 Machines

### 3.1 General Requirements

Manned-capable (side-by-side seats for certification/future demos), but race-remotely-operated only.

**Propulsion:** Series hybrid — fossil fuel combustion unit drives generator → charges accumulator → powers electric ducted fans.

**Fuel Strategy:**

  * • Propane (LPG) is the only fuel permitted for racing events — mandatory for all competitive sessions.
  * • Kerosene is the intended consumer fuel. Diesel is supported for technology transfer, including trucking.
  * • All machines must be homologated as multi-fuel capable.



**Power:** Unlimited — Maximum Boost.

### 3.3 Weight

**Max Recommended Takeoff Weight:** 3500 lbs (1588 kg), ready to race, no pilot, with full 200 lb propane and full ballast.

**Min Dry Weight:** ~2500 lbs (1134 kg).

**Fuel Capacity:** 200 lb propane maximum onboard.

**Ballast:** Each machine must be capable of carrying 500 lb (227 kg) of ballast in the front seats. Ballast weight may be traded for additional instrumentation. The series may reduce the required ballast mass on a case-by-case, vehicle-by-vehicle basis.

### 3.4 Cockpit & Seating

Mandatory: Two side-by-side bare-frame seats (no padding required).

Cockpit remains empty of people during races (may carry sensors or ballast; see Ballast in 3.3).

### 3.5 Control & SI Boost System

**Remote Operation Interface:** All piloting occurs through a gaming interface (PC, Xbox, or PlayStation console with standard game controller).

Minimum requirement: Standard game console controller plus compatible PC/Xbox/PlayStation running the X1Racing interface software. No sim rig required.

AR goggles are permitted as optional visual enhancement. Sim rig interface software is open source.

**Communications:** All communications must route exclusively through the Starlink network. Direct-to-machine links are prohibited. Target end-to-end latency: < 50 ms.

**X1 SI Boost (mandatory, sealed unit):** All external control inputs and flight control sensor data must pass through the SI Boost layer. Each motor is individually controlled through the SI Boost layer.

Core functions: Real-time collision avoidance (Safe Zones only), 3D geofence enforcement, input augmentation, predictive assists, energy optimization, and full telemetry logging.

Tamper-proof: Modification or bypass = disqualification + penalties.

### 3.6 Propulsion & Powertrain

**Architecture:** Series hybrid — fossil fuel combustion unit drives generator → charges accumulator → powers electric motors.

**Generation Unit:** Must demonstrate continuous 50-hour operation at race output for homologation.

**Electric Accumulator:** Maximum 10 kWh nominal capacity.

**3.6.1 Vertical lift.** Each vehicle shall have exactly four (4) ducted lift-fan locations. Each location shall contain one coaxial pair of counter-rotating fans, each driven by its own motor, for a total of eight (8) lift fans and eight (8) lift motors per vehicle.

**3.6.2 Forward flight.** Each vehicle shall have exactly two (2) ducted forward-flight fans.

**3.6.3 Total fan count.** The total fan count is ten (10): eight (8) lift fans per 3.6.1 and two (2) forward-flight fans per 3.6.2.

**3.6.4 Maximum duct diameters.**

  * • Lift duct: 44 in (1.118 m) maximum inner diameter.
  * • Forward-flight duct: 36 in (0.914 m) maximum inner diameter.



**3.6.5 Measurement.** Duct inner diameter is the smallest diameter of the duct bore, measured perpendicular to the fan axis at the plane of the fan blades. A duct may be stationary or may rotate with its fan (blade-tip rim). In either case, its inner diameter is measured at the bore in the plane of the fan blades. If the duct rotates with its fan, its outer diameter shall not exceed the maximum inner diameter in 3.6.4 plus 1 in (25.4 mm). Every fan must turn entirely inside its duct. Fan diameter and tip clearance are not otherwise limited. For a stationary duct, the outer skin, lips, fairings and nacelles are not limited by 3.6.4. They remain subject to the overall dimension limits in 3.2.

**Fuel Capacity:** 200 lb propane maximum onboard. Tanks may be single or multiple.

Power-off landing mandatory (accumulator + regen). Thrust-to-weight: >2:1 encouraged.

### 3.7 Transport & Packaging

**Folded Max Dimensions:** 53" height × 90" width × 18 feet length.

**Hauler Capacity:** Must hold at least 2 folded machines in upper deck.

**Unfolding/Assembly:** Max 20 minutes (1–2 persons). Loading: Solo (1 person). Unloading: Min 2 persons (no time limit).

Folding: Manual permitted; electric actuators optional (game controller if used).

### 3.8 Safety & Redundancy

SI Boost primary safety envelope.

**Automatic Fail-Safes:** Hover, controlled descent, return-to-home.

**Failure Mode Design Requirement (mandatory):** Systems must be designed so that failure modes do not cause collateral damage to spectators ever, or to other competitors where possible.

**Environmental Safety:** Fuels and liquids must be biodegradable and require minimal cleanup.

### 3.9 Homologation

Full inspection/testing required for every machine/update. Proof: the 10-fan layout defined in 3.6.3 (8 coaxial lift fans + 2 forward-flight fans), 50-hour generator endurance, 10 kWh accumulator limit, power-off landing, folded dimensions, timed assembly, remote ops + manned capability, multi-fuel capability (propane mandatory for races, 200 lb maximum onboard; kerosene is the intended consumer fuel, and diesel is supported for technology transfer).

## 4.0 Testing

**Aerial Testing:** Unlimited aerial testing is allowed with permission from X1Racing at official test sites.

▸

**Test Facilities:** Regulatory permission will be fast-tracked and test facilities built by X1Racing or partners that are both over water and over land.

▸

**Public Access:** Where possible, facilities will be dual-use and accessible to be viewed by the public.

## 5.0 On-Track Rules

The X1 SI Boost layer is mandatory. All external control inputs, flight control sensor data, individual fan motors, the generator, and the accumulator are controlled exclusively through the SI Boost layer.

#### Track Zones

Enforced exclusively by the X1 SI Boost layer:

##### Safe Zones

Collisions are prevented. SI automatically overrides inputs to avoid contact.

##### Danger Zones

Collisions are allowed, encouraged, and include deliberate ramming — legal and unrestricted. Major impact, damage, and deliberate targeting are fully permitted. SI authority is reduced, but SI always keeps the authority needed to prevent collateral damage to spectators and property.
